# Kowsar & Ramin Localization Style Guide

## Voice
Warm, capable, human, plain and internationally understandable. The site should sound like real people offering work and collaboration, not a corporation, tourism brochure or machine translation.

## Global rules
- Preserve intent and naturalness over literal word order.
- Prefer common everyday professional vocabulary.
- Avoid jargon, bureaucratic phrasing, inflated claims and marketing superlatives.
- Keep proper names and brand names stable.
- Preserve the compact middle-dot structure of service and opportunity lines.
- Keep CTAs short enough for mobile.
- Translate visible text, metadata, share-sheet content, status messages and accessibility labels together.
- Do not translate URLs, email, phone, QR/UTM identifiers or analytics enums.
- Use NFC Unicode and target-language punctuation conventions.
- Persian uses RTL and Iranian Persian conventions, including appropriate نیم‌فاصله.
- Language names appear in their own language and carry correct language/direction metadata.

## Locale decisions
- **English (`en`)** — source authority.
- **Türkçe (`tr`)** — standard Turkey Turkish; warm and direct.
- **Deutsch (`de`)** — neutral Standard German; clear, not bureaucratic.
- **Español (`es`)** — neutral international Spanish; avoid regional slang.
- **Português (`pt`)** — European Portuguese base with vocabulary chosen for broad comprehension; split `pt-BR` only if evidence later supports it.
- **Italiano (`it`)** — standard Italian; conversational-professional.
- **فارسی (`fa`)** — standard Iranian Persian; formal-neutral warmth; RTL.
- **Français (`fr`)** — international French; standard vocabulary.
- **Azərbaycanca (`az`)** — standard Azerbaijani of Azerbaijan, Latin script.
- **Հայերեն (`hy`)** — standard Eastern Armenian used in Armenia.
- **ქართული (`ka`)** — standard modern Georgian.

## Meaning decisions
- **Together** remains the project/identity label `Together`; do not translate it as an ordinary adverb.
- **Wellbeing** is rendered as the most natural broad non-clinical concept available in each language.
- **Commissions** means paid/custom commissioned work, not sales commission.
- **Bookings** means reservations/bookings for work, music, hospitality or events.
- **Craftsmanship / artisan craft** means skilled handcraft rather than fine art alone.
- The hero line may be idiomatically restructured where literal translation would sound synthetic.
